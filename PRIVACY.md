# Club games privacy policy

Club games does not collect, upload, or share any personal data.

- Games read from your Chessnut board, games you paste or share into the app, and their Stockfish analysis are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- Bluetooth is used only to connect to your Chessnut board. The app does not use your location.
- The release app has no internet permission. Links in the About screen open in your browser.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reporting.

Contact: open an issue on the project's source repository.
