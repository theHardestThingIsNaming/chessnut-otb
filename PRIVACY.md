# Club games privacy policy

Club games has no accounts of its own, ads, analytics or crash reporting. It only talks to Lichess, and only when you use a Lichess feature.

- **On your phone:** Games read from your Chessnut board, games you play, paste or share into the app, their Stockfish analysis, your repertoire and your practice progress are stored only in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Bluetooth:** It is used only to connect to your Chessnut board. The app does not use your location. Older Android versions require the location permission for Bluetooth scanning.
- **Lichess login:** If you log in, the app keeps a Lichess access token, encrypted on the phone. Logging out deletes it and revokes it with Lichess. The app never sees your Lichess password.
- **Data sent to Lichess**, all under your Lichess account and the [Lichess privacy policy](https://lichess.org/privacy):
  - The moves of online games you play.
  - Club games from your board, if "Upload club games to Lichess" is on (it can be turned off in Settings), or when you choose "Upload to Lichess" for a game. Imported games are public on Lichess.
  - Opening positions, to look up win rates in the Lichess opening explorer.
  - Study links you import, to download the study.
- **Nothing else** is sent anywhere. Links in the app open in your browser.
